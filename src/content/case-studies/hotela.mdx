<Problem>

Hotels and guesthouses need software that matches how staff actually work—front desk, housekeeping, and management—not a generic spreadsheet with a logo. Hotela targets hotel operations with a product teams can run daily.

</Problem>

<WhatWouldHaveBroken>

- **Feature sprawl without a clear domain model**—rooms, rates, and bookings fighting each other in the database.
- **Web-only flows** where staff are on their feet with unreliable Wi‑Fi.
- **Billing and guest folios** bolted on late, with no audit trail.
- **Permissions** that treat every user as an admin.

</WhatWouldHaveBroken>
